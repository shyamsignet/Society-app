📢 *Announcement: SociYo, our society's own app, is now live!* 🏡

Dear Residents of *Shyam Signet*,

We're happy to announce *SociYo*, one simple app for everything in our society. No more scrolling through long WhatsApp chats for notices or chasing receipts!

✨ *What you can do on SociYo*
💳 Pay maintenance by UPI and download receipts
🛠️ Raise complaints and track them until they're fixed
🚪 Send QR/OTP gate passes to guests and delivery staff, and approve visitors at the gate
📌 Get committee notices and urgent alerts
🏸 Book the clubhouse, hall and courts with no double booking
🎉 Events, RSVPs and polls on society decisions
🆘 Emergency SOS to alert security in one tap
👥 Resident directory, community feed and marketplace

🌐 Works in *English, मराठी and हिंदी*. You don't need the Play Store or App Store, and it runs on any Android phone or iPhone.

👉 *Open the app:* https://shyamsignet.github.io/Society-app/

📲 *Join in 3 simple steps*
1️⃣ Open the link above in *Chrome* (Android) or *Safari* (iPhone).
2️⃣ Add it to your home screen:
   • Android: tap ⋮ → *Add to Home screen*
   • iPhone: tap *Share* → *Add to Home Screen*
3️⃣ Log in with your *email or mobile number*, enter the code you receive, tap *Join my society*, enter society code *2580* and select your flat.

✅ Once the committee approves your request, you're in! You can also add your family members from *Profile*.

We request *every flat to join this week* so that notices, bills and visitor approvals reach everyone on time. 🙏

For any help, please contact the society office or any committee member.

Warm regards,
*Managing Committee, Shyam Signet*
